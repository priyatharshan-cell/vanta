Questions, answered.

Are the phones brand new and under warranty?

Yes. Every phone is sealed and comes with the manufacturer's warranty plus a local invoice. We never sell refurbished units as new.

What does free setup include?

We activate the SIM, update the system, sign in your accounts, move photos, contacts and chats, and fit a screen protector.

Can I trade in my old phone?

Yes. Bring it in and we quote in five minutes. The credit goes straight toward your new phone, and Club members get an extra bonus.

How fast is delivery?

Island-wide courier delivery takes 24 to 48 hours. You get a tracking link, and the parcel is insured until you sign for it.

Can I cancel Vanta Club?

Any time. Monthly plans stop at the end of the billing period. Yearly plans are refunded pro rata in the first 30 days.
